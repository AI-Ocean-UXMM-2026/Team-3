Four stops between a chat and a doorstep

  1. Paste the morning's chats

    Copy orders from Messenger in a batch. LannPya reads Burmese, Zawgyi and English, and keeps the original message beside what it found.

  2. Check what needs you

    Addresses are matched to 33 Yangon townships. Anything unclear says what is wrong and what to do, so you fix it in one place.

  3. Buyers confirm their own pin

    Each buyer is sent a link to their own portal. They verify with their full phone number and the order ID from the dispatcher, then drop a pin and pick a time. No chasing in chat.

  4. Riders follow the route

    Riders see the next stop by landmark and the exact cash to collect. Close-out compares counted cash with what was recorded.

Three people, one job each

Dispatcher

At the shop counter with Messenger open. Turns a batch of chat orders into checked routes before the vans leave.

Buyer

Opens a link inside the chat app on any Android phone. Confirms where and when in about a minute.

Rider

On a motorbike, outdoors, sometimes offline. Sees one stop at a time and marks it delivered with one tap.

Nothing moves on its own

  • Every send, hold and delivery is a tap by a person.
  • Every flag says the reason, not a code.
  • The buyer's original message stays in view.
  • Work is kept offline and sent when you're back online.
